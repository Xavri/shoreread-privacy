# ShoreRead — privacy policy

The published privacy policy for **ShoreRead** (`com.shoreread.app`), an Android
app that answers one question: can I swim here right now?

- Live page: https://REPLACE-WITH-PAGES-URL/
- Contact: kz.xavri@gmail.com

`index.html` is the whole site — a single self-contained page, English and
Russian, no build step and no third-party requests (system fonts only, so the
privacy page itself loads nothing from anyone).

## Editing

The source of truth for the text lives in the app repository at
`store-assets/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`. When that changes, update `index.html` here
and bump the effective date in both language sections.

Google Play requires the policy URL to stay reachable and to keep describing
what the shipped bundle actually does.
